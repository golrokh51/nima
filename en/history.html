Founded by 43 people, sustained by many friends

Nima Yushij Library began with 43 founders, but its story did not end with that first group. Over the years, other friends joined them, giving their time, effort, and care to keep this home for Persian books and documents alive.

Along the way, some stayed, some left, and some stepped away following disagreements or hurt feelings. Others have passed away, and their memory remains with us. Each, in their own way and at a particular time, has been part of the library’s life.

The library’s fortieth anniversary is an opportunity to recognize all these contributions: those of its founders, the friends who joined later, and those who carry the work forward today. The library grew from a collective beginning and has endured through years of shared care. Many friends have left their mark on this home.

The detailed history and archival photographs will be added as they are gathered and verified.

A milestone this December

In December 2026, Nima Yushij Library marks forty years of preserving Persian-language books and documents.
